# Highlights

- A stair-wear profile identifies only the product of traffic and wear coefficient.
- Without calibration, traffic remains uncertain over six orders of magnitude.
- Calibration on stairs with known traffic narrows the range to about 26%.
- Reference-surface error biases traffic by up to 82% if not modelled.
- A public Roman scan and a Borobudur anchor bound, but do not calibrate, traffic.
